Core capabilities

Make uncertainty operational.

Delivery state should be explicit, recoverable, and tied to evidence—not inferred from a timeout or a green network icon.

01 / Durability

Keep the operation

Persist pending work with a stable identity so reconnects and process interruption do not erase the sender’s intent.

02 / Reconciliation

Resolve before retry

When a response is ambiguous, query the receiver when supported before attempting another side effect.

03 / Evidence

Prove only what is known

Keep transport acceptance, receiver durability, business effect, and final evidence as distinct states.

Operating model

A clear path from intent to evidence.

01

Persist

Record the operation and stable idempotency identity before the network attempt.

02

Deliver or reconcile

Retry under an explicit contract; use receiver-side idempotency and query semantics where available.

03

Report the evidence

Keep UNKNOWN and conflicting evidence visible until the declared outcome can be supported.

Assurance boundaries

Evidence, not optimism.

HTTP success alone does not prove a business effect. Stronger assurance depends on receiver participation, durable idempotency, and an inspectable reconciliation path.

No universal exactly-once guarantee, end-to-end encryption, satellite connectivity, presence, or read-receipt claim is made by this demo.
