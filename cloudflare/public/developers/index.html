Durability without optimistic claims.

The Android runtime persists critical operations, plans around link quality, and reconciles ambiguous outcomes before retry.

  request = request,
  deliveryProfile = DeliveryProfileV2(

  )

Use a stable operation identity and a backend with storage-boundary idempotency. A transport success is not automatically a business completion.

Limits

Zungun V1 does not claim universal exactly-once delivery, E2EE, satellite connectivity, presence or read receipts. Evidence is bounded by participating clients and backend state.
