# Resilience Lab

The deterministic suite covers offline and intermittent transport, delay, timeout, duplication, reordering, truncation, process death, reboot, upgrade, clock skew, TTL expiry, storage failure, receiver crash, lease loss, acknowledgement loss, delayed effect, database/ERP mismatch, receipt alteration, stale and revoked keys, and reconciliation contradictions. Reports include fixed seeds and machine-readable results.
